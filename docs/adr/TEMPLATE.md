# ADR-NNN — Short decision title

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Story: E?-S?

## Context

What forces are at play? What constraint, incident, or requirement makes a decision necessary? State
the facts, not the preference.

## Decision

What we will do. Be specific: protocol, storage, ownership, failure mode. If it is
security-critical, state that it needs explicit sign-off.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what new operational work appears. Include the negative
consequences honestly.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative, and why it was rejected. "Do nothing" is an alternative.
